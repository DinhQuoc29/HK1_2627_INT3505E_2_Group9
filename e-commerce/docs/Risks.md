# Marketplace risk register

Each RSK identifies a condition that could prevent a use case from reaching its intended outcome. The last column gives the team a proposed way to reduce or check that risk. IDs refer to the [requirements](Requirements.md) and [assumptions](Assumptions.md). V1 risks continue to apply in V2.

## V1 — First release

| ID | Risk and impact | Related REQs | Related ASMs | Proposed control or check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RSK-01 | If Google sign-in is unavailable, returning buyers who rely on it may lose access to their accounts and orders. | REQ-01 | ASM-01 | Simulate an unavailable identity provider. Show a retryable error and preserve the account. Offer email sign-in when the buyer already has that method configured. |
| RSK-02 | If logout ends only the visible session, the same session may still open private orders or change account data. | REQ-02 | — | End the session on logout and verify that subsequent protected requests from it are denied. |
| RSK-03 | An incorrect role or shop assignment, or a missing ownership check, could expose another buyer's order or let a seller change another shop's inventory. | REQ-03, REQ-05, REQ-16 | ASM-02, ASM-04 | Verify role assignments and check ownership for reads and changes. Test with two buyers, two shops, and an account without admin permission. |
| RSK-04 | A broken category hierarchy or incorrect brand or province data could hide a valid listing from buyers or include it in the wrong search results. | REQ-04, REQ-05, REQ-06, REQ-07 | ASM-03, ASM-11 | Reject cyclic category assignments. Check browsing and combined filters against known listings, including one without a brand. |
| RSK-05 | A seller may report a damaged unit or an offline sale late. The platform could accept an order even though its recorded stock exceeds the physical stock. | REQ-05, REQ-07, REQ-13 | ASM-05 | Have sellers reconcile available units after offline changes. Verify that a correction to zero prevents subsequent purchases. |
| RSK-06 | If ranking times out or a new buyer has no history, suggestions may disappear. A stale popular list could also recommend a withdrawn, sold-out, or over-budget item. | REQ-08, REQ-09 | ASM-03, ASM-05, ASM-06 | Exercise both the new-buyer and timeout paths. Check fallback results against current listings and the per-item budget, including an exact-budget item and a case with no eligible items. |
| RSK-07 | If a device or recommendation component uses an outdated personalization preference, the marketplace may keep recording activity or using history after a buyer opts out. | REQ-10 | ASM-07 | Turn personalization off on one device and shop on another. Verify both that new personalization events stop and that suggestions no longer use history. |
| RSK-08 | A cart may contain an old price or a withdrawn listing. The buyer could confirm a different total from the one they reviewed or purchase an unavailable item. | REQ-07, REQ-11, REQ-12 | ASM-03, ASM-04, ASM-05, ASM-06 | Change a price and withdraw a listing after adding them to a cart. Verify fresh confirmation for the revised price and rejection of the withdrawn item. |
| RSK-09 | Concurrent buyers could both obtain the last unit. A failure midway through multi-shop checkout could leave one seller order or stock deduction behind after the purchase fails. | REQ-13, REQ-14 | ASM-04, ASM-05, ASM-08 | Coordinate the stock change and purchase records in one transaction. Test two buyers competing for one unit and a checkout that fails on the second shop. Inspect all orders, stock, and payment records afterward. |
| RSK-10 | A lost checkout response may prompt a retry that creates a second purchase. Incorrect shop allocations may also make the simulated payment disagree with the orders. | REQ-13, REQ-14 | ASM-04, ASM-06, ASM-08 | Retry the same confirmation after a successful purchase whose response was lost. Verify one payment, one order per shop, one deduction per item, and allocations that sum to the total. |
| RSK-11 | If order history uses live listing data, a seller's rename, price change, or withdrawal could alter what buyers see for a completed purchase. | REQ-15 | ASM-03, ASM-04 | Record the purchased name and unit price. Edit and then withdraw the listing and compare the order details with the original confirmation. |
| RSK-12 | A late dispatch update or an undefined cancellation cutoff could let a buyer cancel an order already in transit. Repeated cancellation could restore stock twice or adjust the wrong shop's allocation. | REQ-16, REQ-17 | ASM-04, ASM-08, ASM-09, ASM-10 | Agree on the cutoff with sellers. Test cancellation before and after it, simultaneous dispatch and cancellation, and a repeated cancellation in a two-shop purchase. Check both shops' resulting states. |
| RSK-17 | A ranker can retrieve the right products yet bury the best matches. Tuning on evaluation queries or comparing different candidate sets can make ML appear better than the baseline. | REQ-06 | ASM-03, ASM-16 | Reserve labeled queries before tuning. Compare both rankers on identical candidates and filters, inspect queries where ML performs worse, and verify the baseline still serves search during a model outage. |

## V2 — Deferred features

| ID | Risk and impact | Related REQs | Related ASMs | Proposed control or check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RSK-13 | The chatbot may misread a budget, suggest an ineligible listing, or claim a feature the seller never supplied. A buyer could choose an unsuitable product based on the explanation. | REQ-09, REQ-18 | ASM-03, ASM-06, ASM-12 | Test ambiguous budgets, missing attributes, and requests with no match. Ask for clarification where needed and check each suggested link and factual claim against the eligible listing. |
| RSK-14 | If dispatch is treated as completion, the wrong buyer is matched to an item, or a submission is repeated, an ineligible or duplicate rating could affect other shoppers. | REQ-19 | ASM-04, ASM-13 | Define completion with the fulfillment team. Test dispatched and completed orders, a different buyer, a repeated submission, and an attempt to edit another purchaser's review. |
| RSK-15 | Stale aggregates can leave edited or removed ratings visible in search. Treating an unrated product as zero stars can also distort rating filters. | REQ-20, REQ-21 | ASM-14 | Agree on how filters treat unrated products. Check the listing and search results after an edit, after moderation, and after the last published rating is removed. |
| RSK-16 | A report may identify the wrong review or listing, or admins may lack criteria for a decision. Legitimate content could be removed while the reported content remains available. | REQ-21, REQ-22 | ASM-15, ASM-04 | Agree on moderation criteria and show the exact reported item with its context. Verify retain and remove decisions affect only that item. For listing withdrawals, verify checkout is blocked and past order details are preserved. |
